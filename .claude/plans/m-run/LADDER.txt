
=== THE LADDER, PER MODE (600 runs/cell, COMPETENT shipped kit) ===
low-water = the lowest fraction of maxHP Andrew ever stood at, averaged over
            runs, with a defeat counted as 0. near-death = share of runs whose
            low-water reached 25 %. These are the producer-facing pair; win rate
            saturates long before a fight stops being frightening.
quiet     = enemy turns that landed and dealt 0 damage. dotT is the part of that
            which is a DoT being APPLIED (the damage lands two beats later on
            Andrew's turn-start) — subtract it to read the real bookkeeping turns.
