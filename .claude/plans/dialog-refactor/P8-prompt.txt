=== THE BUG CLASS, STATED ONCE ===
Every code-side story push in ExplorationState spends its once-latch BEFORE the dialog it
guards has run — `setFlag(started)` and then `setTimeout(push, 800-2000 ms)` — and the game
AUTO-SAVES inside that window. A quit, a crash or a closed tab banks "this scene has
happened" for a scene that did not happen, and because none of these enemies has an NPC
entry anywhere in the game, nothing can ever push that dialog again. Measured 2026-08-15 by
tools/_r-act5latch.mjs on the worst case: an interrupted cubicle-farm entry banks
act5_triggered:true / act4_complete:false, and Acts 5, 6 and 7 become unreachable for the
life of the save.

The PREVENTION (DESIGN 4.4): the once-predicate stops being a hand-written flag and becomes
the scene's own `read_<sceneId>`, which DialogState._endDialog writes ONLY after a node has
actually been shown (DialogState.js:~466-473). An interrupted scene is then re-servable by
construction.
